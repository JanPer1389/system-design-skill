# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss proposals and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or intimidation. Respect privacy and do not post credentials or confidential project material.

Keep issues and pull requests focused on this package. Explain observed behavior, the expected outcome and the evidence needed to assess a change. Acknowledge uncertainty and correct inaccurate claims.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through GitHub's available reporting tools. Avoid exposing private details in public issues.
